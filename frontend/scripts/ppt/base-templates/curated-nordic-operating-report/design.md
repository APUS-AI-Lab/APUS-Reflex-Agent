# Nordic Operating Report · WorkBuddy Original DESIGN SYSTEM

This original 16:9 system turns management evidence into a restrained annual operating report. Warm paper, deep steel blue, mist-blue rules, pale-clay risk cues, precise grids, and direct data placement create a formal reading experience without dashboard decoration. Every visible item in the reference deck is an editable PPTD object.

## Communication model

Use this system for annual operating reviews, international expansion, supply-chain decisions, market-entry portfolios, capability planning, and management action decks.

1. Lead each page with a management judgment rather than a neutral topic label.
2. Place years, units, updated-statistics labels, public-data dates, and derived-calculation notes beside the evidence.
3. Give each page one primary reading structure: a ledger, split ratio, concentration comparison, operating loop, decision matrix, capability register, or investment staircase.
4. Use cyan for the current signal, pale clay for risk or irreversible commitment, and deep steel blue for the operating system.
5. Retain a quiet source line and page register on analytical pages.

Normal analytical pages keep warm paper or mist blue as the dominant field. Navy pages act as structural pauses for export, fulfilment, entry, and action. Cyan marks a current signal or active path. Clay marks concentration risk, reverse logistics, or increased commitment.

## Typography and grid

- Chinese: `HarmonyOS Sans SC`, with a compatible system CJK sans-serif fallback.
- Latin labels and large metrics: `Arial`.
- Canvas: PPTD v2, `960 × 540`, 16:9.
- Default outer margin: 46–52 px, with a 10–14 px left registration rail.
- Page titles: 31–34 px, bold, left aligned.
- Running labels: 8–9 px uppercase Latin with modest tracking.
- Body and evidence notes: 8.5–11.5 px with short lines and visible grouping.
- Use square geometry, hairline rules, direct chart placement, and clear column axes. Avoid rounded-card walls, gradients, glow, soft shadows, and decorative 3D objects.

## Reusable components

1. **Registration rail**: one narrow left strip that identifies the report family and stabilizes page alignment.
2. **Operating ledger**: a native chart plus a metric register and one full-width evidence strip.
3. **Ratio field**: one uninterrupted horizontal proportion with direct labels for export/import or comparable structures.
4. **Concentration comparison**: mirrored market bars with country labels, totals, and one explicit risk interpretation.
5. **Operating loop**: simple native nodes connected by a line, followed by one cross-cutting control band.
6. **Decision register**: a native table or ruled rows comparing responsibility, investment, output, and review frequency.
7. **Commitment staircase**: progressively taller fields representing increasing local control and irreversible cost.

## Charts and tables

- Charts sit directly on the page field without a floating card or shadow.
- Use navy for structural series, cyan for the current signal, mist for secondary context, and clay for risk or commitment.
- Keep axes minimal, grid lines fine, and units visible near the chart title.
- Tables use a navy header, paper cells, hairline borders, and no alternating decorative fill.
- When a derived value appears, label it as calculated from disclosed customs data.

## Page rhythm

The ten-page reference deck covers a thesis cover, executive operating ledger, scale trajectory, export structure, market concentration, overseas-warehouse loop, channel portfolio, capability system, market-entry staircase, and audience-specific actions. Warm analytical pages alternate with navy structural pages. The same grid and furniture recur, while the dominant visual form changes with each management question.

## Prohibited treatments

- Generic icon walls, stock-photo heroes, and decorative dashboards.
- Rounded rectangles as the default grouping device.
- Blue-purple gradients, glass effects, glow, soft shadows, and 3D charts.
- Large decorative circles or abstract AI-generated ornaments.
- Rasterized text, charts, tables, arrows, or process diagrams that PPTD can represent natively.
