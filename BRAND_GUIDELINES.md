# APUS Reflex brand guidelines

English | [中文](BRAND_GUIDELINES.zh.md)

Use **APUS Reflex** as the product name in interfaces, welcome messages, model identity, documentation, diagrams and application displays. “Harness” describes the runtime and “Desktop” describes its desktop client; they are component labels, not separate product brands.

Use the APUS mark and assets described in [brand and assembly](docs/feat-apus-brand/branding.md). Do not use inherited product marks to imply an upstream endorsement.

Package names, provider names, persisted identifiers, application IDs and existing user-data paths retain their compatibility meaning. They are not product display names. Preserve the original [license](LICENSE) and [third-party notices](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md); a brand change does not transfer authorship or ownership of dependencies.
